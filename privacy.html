Privacy policy

Deepak's 10X Life · Updated 15 Nov 2026

This app is used by members of Deepak's 10X Life programs to track daily habits with their coach.

What we keep

Your name, email and phone number (given to your coach when you join), your habit ticks and points, your goals, notes, questions, to-dos and pictures you add, and your journal entries.

Google login

If you log in with Google, we receive only your name, email address and profile picture from Google. We use them to find the account your coach created for you. We do not read your Gmail, contacts or any other Google data.

Who sees it

Your coach sees your ticks, points, notes, questions and pictures. Other members see only your name, profile picture and progress (points, habits done, streak) on the leaderboard. Your journal stays private unless you choose to share an entry with your coach.

We do not sell or share your data with anyone else. It is stored securely with Supabase (our database provider).

Removing your data

Ask your coach and your account and data will be deleted.
